Who We Are

Molo's Go-To Venue for Events & Stays

Greenland Holiday Centre is a well-established, locally-rooted venue in Molo, Nakuru County. We specialise in providing a conducive, professional environment for conferences, workshops, training sessions, community meetings and events — while also offering comfortable bed and breakfast accommodation.

Whether you are an NGO running a training programme, a government body hosting a workshop, a business team holding a planning session, or a community group organising a gathering — we have the space, the support and the experience to make it work.

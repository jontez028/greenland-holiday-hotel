About the Hotel

A Highland Haven
in the Heart of Molo

Greenland Holiday Hotel offers warm, comfortable accommodation in the heart of Molo town. Whether you're travelling for business, attending an event, or exploring the Rift Valley, we provide a clean, peaceful and welcoming home away from home.

We take pride in our friendly staff, freshly prepared meals, and the naturally cool highland climate that makes Molo one of Kenya's most refreshing destinations.

Who We Are

Molo's Most Trusted Event Venue

Greenland Holiday Centre is Molo's established home for conferences, workshops, trainings and events. We serve businesses, NGOs, government bodies, conservation organisations and community groups who need a professional, well-run venue in Nakuru County.

Whether it's a half-day workshop for 15 or a multi-day residential training for 60 — we provide the space, the food, the accommodation and the support to make it work.

Dining & Catering

Our Restaurant & Event Kitchen

Our restaurant is one of Molo's most distinctive — marked by iconic hand-carved giraffe sculptures at the entrance and a warm, stone-floored dining area with wicker chairs. We serve freshly prepared Kenyan meals for all guests and event delegates.

Bed & breakfast is included for overnight guests. Full catering packages for conferences — including morning tea, lunch and dinner — are available on request.
